Since 1984 · The Sunil Jain Legacy

A belief, kept for four decades.

In 1984, Mr. Sunil Jain began with a simple belief — to offer jewellery of exceptional design, with uncompromising purity and honest value. What began in a single room grew, through generations of trust, into a name cherished across Chhattisgarh.

Today, Saheli Aurum carries that legacy forward: more refined, more contemporary, yet deeply rooted in everything that made Saheli what it is.
